Use the supplied chair and moon sources to build a believable Moon-as-Balloon composite, then add an intentional product-ad or social-message layer. The earlier phone portal is the masking preflight, not the final assignment.

Assignment duration: three full 70-minute classes. Do not collapse the lighting build or the transfer challenge into filler time.

Three-class sprint

Class A · Diagnose, select and constructGallery diagnosis and a short phone-mask preflight lead into source compatibility, Moon selection, Smart Object placement, transform/distort and the first rough composite.
Class B · Make the impossible believableIsolate the chair, rebuild contact and cast shadows, create the day-for-night grade and paint local moonlight on the chair, supports, seat and ribbon.
Class C · Communicate, test and transferAdd glow/rim light, complete a believability audit, add a purposeful campaign message, revise after critique and prove one unpredictable live edit.

Pathways that add real complexity

Tier 1Guided buildComplete the supplied Moon-as-Balloon composite with editable masks, coherent geometry, local light and shadow.
Tier 2Creative variationChange the scale, placement, crop or colour world while preserving believable interaction and a clear message.
Tier 3Independent campaignTransfer the workflow to new rights-safe assets and create an original surreal product advertisement or social-issue image.

Required evidence

  1. Concept thumbnail and source-compatibility gate
  2. Rough composite screenshot with Layers panel visible
  3. Midpoint screenshot showing isolated local-light and shadow layers
  4. Final layered PSD with named masks, adjustments and Smart Objects
  5. Final campaign PNG plus before/after comparison
  6. Source and asset log
  7. Production defence and successful live-edit challenge
