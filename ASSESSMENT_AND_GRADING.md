# Assessment + Grading — Surreal Composite

## Graded evidence
Students submit:
1. `01_assets` screenshot showing source files.
2. concept thumbnail or rough comp.
3. midpoint screenshot with layers panel visible.
4. final layered PSD.
5. final JPG/PNG export.
6. before/after image or screenshot.
7. production defence.

## Question grading
### Visual diagnosis questions — 3 points each
- 1 point: identifies the visual problem.
- 1 point: explains why it breaks believability.
- 1 point: proposes a technically valid repair.

### Procedure questions — 4 points each
- 1 point: correct tool/layer choice.
- 1 point: correct sequence.
- 1 point: explains why the step matters.
- 1 point: includes checkpoint/expected result.

## Project rubric
| Area | 4 Studio Ready | 3 Production | 2 Developing | 1 Foundation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Technical execution | controlled, editable, accurate | mostly controlled | partial control | limited |
| Visual integration | coherent light/shadow/tone/colour | minor tells | several conflicts | not believable |
| Process evidence | clear iterative workflow | adequate evidence | limited evidence | little evidence |
| Decision-making | explains and defends choices | explains most | describes only | cannot explain |
| Transfer | applies to new assets | applies with help | follows example | cannot transfer |
| AI transparency | clear human vs AI contribution | mostly clear | incomplete | authorship unclear |

## AI-resistant design
This is not AI-proof. It is AI-resistant because students cannot pass with only a finished JPEG. They must show process, layer structure, checkpoints and defend production decisions.

## Live teacher challenge
Ask one unpredictable adjustment: move the Moon, warm the light, change shadow direction, shrink the Moon, or create a second light source. Student explains or demonstrates the required repair.
