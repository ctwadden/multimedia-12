# Surreal Composite Campaign — analytic rubric

| Criterion | Outcome | 1 Beginning | 2 Developing | 3 Secure | 4 Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Non-destructive construction | 1.1 / 1.3 | Layers or evidence are missing and edits are destructive. | Core masks work but organization or repairability is inconsistent. | Named masks, Smart Objects and adjustments remain editable throughout. | The structure is efficient, transferable and supports a confident live edit. |
| Believable integration | 1.1 / 1.4 | Geometry, edges, light or shadow contradict the scene. | The premise reads but several visual systems disagree. | Geometry, value, colour, light, shadow, texture and interaction mostly agree. | Every cue reinforces a deliberate surreal reality with controlled local detail. |
| Communication and hierarchy | 1.5 / 4.2 | Audience or intended message is unclear. | A general message appears but hierarchy competes with the image. | Image and type communicate a clear idea to a named audience. | The campaign creates a memorable interpretation and adapts convincingly to a new context. |
| Process, revision and defence | 1.3 | Required evidence is missing or cannot establish authorship. | Some checkpoints exist; explanations mainly describe. | Checkpoints, critique, before/after and defence explain purposeful decisions. | Evidence shows diagnosis, transfer, precise revision and fluent live control. |
