Audience, purpose and success

This is one cumulative three-class production assignment. Class 1 establishes selection and geometry, Class 2 adds physically coherent light and shadow, and Class 3 demands communication, critique and transfer.

  • Submit the editable layered file, not only a flattened export.
  • Use rights-safe or teacher-supplied assets and keep a source log.
  • Capture required process evidence at the named checkpoints.
  • Complete a silent audience/peer test and one evidence-led revision.
  • Defend one decision and perform one live edit.

Class-by-class milestones

Class A · Diagnose, select and constructGallery diagnosis and a short phone-mask preflight lead into source compatibility, Moon selection, Smart Object placement, transform/distort and the first rough composite.
Class B · Make the impossible believableIsolate the chair, rebuild contact and cast shadows, create the day-for-night grade and paint local moonlight on the chair, supports, seat and ribbon.
Class C · Communicate, test and transferAdd glow/rim light, complete a believability audit, add a purposeful campaign message, revise after critique and prove one unpredictable live edit.

Pathways that add real complexity

Tier 1 · Guided build

Complete the supplied Moon-as-Balloon composite with editable masks, coherent geometry, local light and shadow.

Tier 2 · Creative variation

Change the scale, placement, crop or colour world while preserving believable interaction and a clear message.

Tier 3 · Independent campaign

Transfer the workflow to new rights-safe assets and create an original surreal product advertisement or social-issue image.

Required evidence

Production / inquiry defence

  1. Show the exact evidence behind your strongest decision.
  2. Name one failure or limitation and demonstrate the repair or qualification.
  3. Respond to one unpredictable teacher prompt without rehearsed wording.
  4. Explain what you would transfer to a new asset, audience, format or case.

Submission route

  1. Finish Parts 1–4 and answer the student checks.
  2. Open every file before submitting.
  3. Use the rubric to name one secure criterion and one revision target.
  4. Submit the final product, editable/process evidence and defence record together.
