# Surreal Composite Campaign

Use the supplied chair and moon sources to build a believable Moon-as-Balloon composite, then add an intentional product-ad or social-message layer. The earlier phone portal is the masking preflight, not the final assignment.

## Milestones

- **Class A · Diagnose, select and construct:** Gallery diagnosis and a short phone-mask preflight lead into source compatibility, Moon selection, Smart Object placement, transform/distort and the first rough composite.
- **Class B · Make the impossible believable:** Isolate the chair, rebuild contact and cast shadows, create the day-for-night grade and paint local moonlight on the chair, supports, seat and ribbon.
- **Class C · Communicate, test and transfer:** Add glow/rim light, complete a believability audit, add a purposeful campaign message, revise after critique and prove one unpredictable live edit.
