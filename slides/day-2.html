MM12 · Mechanism

How it actually works

A pixel mask stores visibility as grayscale. White reveals the layer, black conceals it and gray creates partial transparency.

MM12 · Pathways

Support and extension

Support

Begin from Select Subject and refine one wing plus one antenna only.

Extension

Unlink the mask, move the image inside it, then explain why unlinking can help a frame-based composite.

Decode the Impossible
Speaker notes

Support changes access to the same essential skill. It does not hide the target technique.

MM12 · Evidence

Checkpoint and exit

Checkpoint

The butterfly has an editable mask; the image thumbnail is untouched; edge test screenshot shows the mask and one problem you repaired.

Exit question

Explain the difference between selecting pixels and masking a layer.

Speaker notes
