# Student Questions + Answer Key

## Visual diagnosis
1. A subject has a perfect mask but the shadow points toward the light source. What is wrong and how do you fix it?
**Key:** shadow direction is physically inconsistent; rebuild/rotate the cast shadow so it projects away from the light; keep contact shadow near the object.

2. The subject looks pasted on even though colour seems similar. Name two other systems to inspect.
**Key:** scale/perspective, light direction, tone/contrast, edge softness/fringe, texture/grain, interaction/shadow.

3. Why is a contact shadow usually darker and harder than the far end of a cast shadow?
**Key:** contact shadow is where light is most occluded at the surface contact; cast shadow softens/fades with distance and light size.

## Procedure
4. How would you add moonlight to the chair seat without permanently painting on the original image?
**Key:** create adjustment layer such as Curves/Color Balance or use mask on grade; invert/hide mask; select/paint seat area; use low opacity; keep non-destructive.

5. Why might you use Harmonize, then still manually edit the result?
**Key:** Harmonize can match tone/colour/shadow quickly, but it may not solve geometry, creative intent, exact local highlights, masks, or all interactions.

## Defence prompts
- Which layer makes the Moon feel physically connected to the chair?
- Which layer would you turn off to reveal the biggest weakness?
- What did you fix after peer critique?
- What could AI do here, and what must you still judge?
