# Photoshop Foundations — Phone Portal + Out-of-Bounds Extension

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox. No server, login, package install, or internet connection is required for the core lesson.

## Ready-to-teach flow

- 10–12 min: original eight-image gallery walk
- 8 min: technique decoder
- 7 min: Photoshop workspace map
- 8 min: interactive layers/mask simulator
- 35–45 min: Tier 1 linear “Step Outside” phone-portal build
- 25–35 min: Tier 2 out-of-bounds butterfly extension (same block, early finish, or next class)
- 10 min: eight-question retrieval quiz and exit ticket

Use **Teacher mode** in the top-right corner to reveal pacing notes, answers, checkpoints, and evidence guidance. Use browser Print to make a paper/PDF copy if needed.

## Student files

Students can download `photoshop-foundations-student-files.zip` directly from the lesson’s **Get the files** section. It contains:

- `01-phone-base.png`
- `02-forest-insert.png`
- `03-butterfly-source.png` for Tier 2
- Tier 1 target reference: `target-exemplar.png`
- Tier 2 target reference: `target-exemplar-out-of-bounds.png`

`butterfly-cutout-demo.png` powers the browser simulator; students should use the gray-background source so they practise Select Subject and Select and Mask.

The Tier 2 simulator is now a five-stage mask lab. It shows the original rectangular source, a subject selection, the resulting black/white mask, live black/white brush repair, and separate layer visibility/order. It is a conceptual model of Photoshop, not a substitute for completing the PSD.

Students create:

```text
Photoshop_Foundations_YourName/
  01_assets/
  02_working/
  03_exports/
  04_evidence/
```

## Tier structure

1. **Tier 1 — Foundations:** build the editable phone portal in Steps 0–10.
2. **Tier 2 — Out of Bounds:** cut out the provided butterfly, refine its mask, cross the phone bezel, and tuck one small edge behind it in Steps 11–17.
3. **Tier 3 — Transfer:** replace the butterfly with a rights-safe subject and defend the edge, layer-order, and shadow decisions.

Suggested follow-on lessons: focused edge diagnosis, contact/cast shadows, colour matching, then an independent product or social-issue composite with a source log.

The two generated source images and exemplar are included for classroom practice. The external campaign links are optional comparison material and should be previewed by the teacher under local school policy.
