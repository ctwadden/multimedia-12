Layers before effects

What must stay editable?

Open slides

TEACH

Canvas, toolbar, options bar, Layers panel, visibility, order, naming, Smart Objects and PSD versus export.

HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS

2Day 2 · 70 minutes

Masks conceal without deleting

How can an edge stay repairable?

Open slides
HOOK

Compare the same butterfly on white, black and saturated backgrounds. Look for halos, missing antennae and hard edges.

TEACH

Object Selection, Select Subject, Select and Mask, Overlay view, Refine Edge Brush, Output to Layer Mask, black conceals and white reveals.

HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS

A pixel mask stores visibility as grayscale. White reveals the layer, black conceals it and gray creates partial transparency.

3Day 3 · 70 minutes

The phone becomes a portal

Which clues make the inserted world feel contained by the phone?

Open slides
HOOK

View the target for five seconds, hide it, then list the visual clues that make the forest appear inside the screen.

TEACH

Transform, perspective, screen selection, layer mask, edge light and restrained tonal adjustment.

HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS

The phone bezel supplies an occluding frame. The portal reads when the forest follows the screen perspective and stops exactly at that boundary.

4Day 4 · 70 minutes

Out of bounds

How can one subject appear both inside and outside a frame?

Open slides
HOOK

Study three overlap diagrams and predict which parts must sit above or below the phone bezel.

TEACH

Layer duplication or grouped masks, crossing and tucked edges, contact shadow, colour match and depth cues.

HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS

The effect needs contradictory depth evidence: most of the subject belongs to the portal, while one part occludes the bezel. Separate masks or duplicated layers control the two depth zones.

Polish and defend

Which revision makes the illusion hold together?

Open slides
HOOK

Run a silent two-minute gallery. Reviewers place one note at the least believable edge or lighting clue.

TEACH

Edge audit, grayscale value check, colour check, shadow logic, web export, source log and production defence.

HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS

Viewers detect inconsistencies across edge, perspective, value, colour, light and shadow. A final audit isolates each cue before judging the full composition.

Three-class assignment sprint

Decode the Impossible Composite

The full foundation sequence is five classes. The assessed anchor product is deliberately scoped to three production classes and can be compressed to two by combining the final test with Class B.

1. Class A · Construct

Build the layered phone portal, preserve Smart Objects and create the screen mask. Finish with a Layers-panel checkpoint.

2. Class B · Break the frame

Add the masked butterfly, create one crossing edge and one tucked edge, then integrate it with tone and a contact shadow.

3. Class C · Prove and deliver

Run edge, value, lighting and peer-believability tests; revise once; export and complete the production defence.

Retrieval check

Can you explain the decisions?

1. Which mask colour conceals a layer?

Answer: Black conceals; white reveals.

2. Which file normally preserves layers?

Answer: PSD preserves the editable Photoshop structure.

3. What should a contact shadow do?

Answer: A contact shadow is small and sits where surfaces nearly meet.

4. Why test a cut-out on several backgrounds?

Answer: Different values and colours expose halos or missing detail.

5. What makes the out-of-bounds effect read?

Answer: A crossing edge comes forward while a tucked edge goes behind the frame.

Complete all questions, then check your understanding.
