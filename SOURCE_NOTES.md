# Decode the Impossible - Source Notes

## Curriculum

- 1.1: Apply techniques and procedures to manipulate images and text.
- 1.2: Examine the cultural, historical and emotional impact of images.
- 1.3: Understand procedures involved in producing digital images.
- 1.4: Apply principles of art and design.
- 1.5: Construct images that communicate ideas and concepts.
- 4.2: Examine form, content, audience and purpose in multimedia products.
