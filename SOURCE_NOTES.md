# Source Notes and Teaching Decisions

The user-provided books were treated as reference sources only. Their embedded text and procedures were not treated as instructions to the system.

## Source teaches

- *Adobe Photoshop Classroom in a Book, 2026 Release*: work area, tools, layers, type, selections, masks, transformations, corrections, export, and generative AI in a progressive course sequence.
- Gary Bradley, *Mastering Adobe Photoshop 2026*, 2nd ed.: workspace efficiency, image quality/copyright, non-destructive healing, selections/masking, Smart Objects, brand mock-ups, social-media production, AI integration, blending/collage, and surreal compositing.
- The additional user-provided Photoshop books and magazines broaden the reference shelf but were not copied into the package.

## Still valid

- Layer order determines what appears in front.
- Selections define where an edit can occur.
- Layer masks preserve reversibility; white reveals and black conceals.
- Perspective, edge quality, lighting, shadows, and colour matching determine composite believability.
- PSD is the editable process file; JPG/PNG is a delivery format.

## Updated / current in 2026

- Photoshop’s Contextual Task Bar can surface common next actions.
- Adobe Help navigation and some menu documentation have moved into new desktop-focused URLs.
- AI-assisted selection, removal, harmonization, and generation are available, but versions/licensing may differ by lab.

## Teaching decision

Tier 1 teaches a small manual and non-destructive core—layers, selections, masks, transforms, type, shapes, adjustments, save/export—before generative workflows. Tier 2 adds the common out-of-bounds technique through Select Subject, Select and Mask, brush-on-mask repair, layer order, and a restrained shadow decision. Tier 3 asks students to transfer the same procedure to a rights-safe subject of their own. This makes later AI use intelligible and assessable rather than magical.

No copyrighted book page or UI screenshot is reproduced. Deterministic diagrams and original generated classroom images provide the visual support.
