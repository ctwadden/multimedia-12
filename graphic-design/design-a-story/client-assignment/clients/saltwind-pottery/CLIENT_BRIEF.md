# Saltwind Pottery — fictional teaching client

Saltwind began as a two-person pottery studio. Visitors admire the finished vessels but often assume everything is for sale. Its new introductory session invites people to shape a small clay object themselves. Jonah wants to attract beginners who may think they are “not artistic”.

Audience: Local beginners aged 16 and up who are curious about making but have never visited a ceramics studio.

Problem: Make participation and beginner access visible; avoid a product-catalogue reading.

Approved copy:
- Your first clay workshop
- Saturday · 1–3 pm
- Saltwind Pottery · 8 Studio Row
- Beginners welcome. Materials included.
- Book a place at the studio desk.

Creative choices: You may communicate through process, texture, a surprising object relationship, expressive type or original drawing. The brief does not require a picture of hands.

Constraints: Do not promise a finished fired vessel on the day, describe the course as free, or label an unrelated source pot as made by this fictional studio.

Deliver editable print/screen sources, viewing PNGs, planning and decision/source records. No fixed composition. See the supplied criteria.
