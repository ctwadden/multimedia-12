# Coast Discovery — fictional teaching client

Coast Discovery is a fictional shoreline learning centre. Its earlier publicity looked like a scenic tourism ad: people liked the ocean photograph but could not say what they would do. Leah wants curiosity about noticing small shoreline details without implying scientific claims that the centre has not established.

Audience: Families and older teens interested in a calm guided introduction to shoreline observation.

Problem: Show a learning encounter, rather than simply an attractive destination.

Approved copy:
- Shoreline discovery walk
- Saturday · 10–11:30 am
- Meet at Coast Discovery · 6 Shore Road
- Look closely. Ask questions.
- Wear sturdy shoes. Stay with the group.

Creative choices: You may use scale, detail, a lens metaphor, type, a specimen-style original illustration or a landscape. No prescribed environmental slogan.

Constraints: Do not identify a species from an unverified image, make environmental impact claims, or present the source coast as the real event location. The session is fictional.

Deliver editable print/screen sources, viewing PNGs, planning and decision/source records. No fixed composition. See the supplied criteria.
