# CivicCycle — fictional teaching client

CivicCycle is a fictional community repair group. Its volunteers love bicycles, but its old flyer listed tools and jargon before explaining the event. New riders thought they needed expertise or expensive equipment. Sam wants a clear, friendly invitation to a basic maintenance session.

Audience: First-time riders and families who want to learn a simple maintenance check.

Problem: Make beginner participation approachable while keeping the practical event details clear.

Approved copy:
- Bike care for beginners
- Sunday · 10 am–noon
- CivicCycle · Community Hall, 22 Market Street
- Bring your bicycle. Tools provided.
- Drop in. No experience needed.

Creative choices: Choose energetic, calm, diagram-led, photographic or typographic communication. A complex bike cutout is optional; clean reasoning matters more than a difficult mask.

Constraints: Do not guarantee safety of a repaired bike, promise professional certification, or imply an identifiable stock-photo person endorses the group.

Deliver editable print/screen sources, viewing PNGs, planning and decision/source records. No fixed composition. See the supplied criteria.
