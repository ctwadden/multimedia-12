# Harbour Bean — fictional teaching client

Mara has run a neighbourhood café for seven years. Customers know the morning takeaway queue, but few know that a quiet reading corner is now open in the evening. A previous photograph of a paper cup reinforced the wrong message. Mara needs an invitation that makes staying feel possible.

Audience: Nearby residents and older teens who want a relaxed place to read after school or work.

Problem: Communicate the evening reading experience, rather than just another coffee offer.

Approved copy:
- Evening reading at Harbour Bean
- Thursday · 6–8 pm
- Harbour Bean · 14 Wharf Lane
- Bring a book. Take a seat.
- No booking required. Drinks sold separately.

Creative choices: You may use an atmosphere, a meaningful object, a typographic invitation, an original illustration or a combination. Warmth does not require a brown poster.

Constraints: Do not imply that drinks are free, that the space is a library, or that the supplied synthetic café is a real venue.

Deliver editable print/screen sources, viewing PNGs, planning and decision/source records. No fixed composition. See the supplied criteria.
