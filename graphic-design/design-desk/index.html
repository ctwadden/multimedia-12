Four films. Four visual arguments.

Look closely. Name the design choice, point to evidence, and explain its effect. There can be more than one defensible interpretation.

Start with two posters. For each, discuss two principles; then compare how typography and colour suggest a different kind of film. Multimedia 12: also propose a change and explain its likely trade-off.

Principles and a useful response structure

Emphasis: focal attention. Hierarchy: order of notice. Contrast: meaningful difference. Balance: distribution of visual weight. Movement: a path for the eye. Repetition/rhythm: recurring elements. Alignment/proximity: visual organization. Unity/variety: coherence with purposeful differences. Negative space: areas that give content room.

“The poster uses principle. I can see this in precise feature/location. This helps communicate message/genre because reason. A possible alternative is change, but that could trade-off.”

Use this as a thinking frame, not a list of words to paste into every response. Your explanation needs visible evidence.
