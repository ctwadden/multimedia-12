Multimedia 12 · Week 1 · Part 2

Build geometry + masks

Class 1 · 25–70 min · Follow every step in order. Do not skip the expected-result check.

Before you begin: Open the student ZIP, place it in a named course folder, preserve the originals, and save a versioned working file before editing.
Linear build

Do → inspect → repair

Every visible outcome is taught below. A step is not finished until your screen or evidence matches the expected result.

Step 6

Scale and distort into the balloon role

Edit > Free Transform > Distort/Warp

Hotkey: Cmd/Ctrl T

Expected result
Moon covers the balloon and respects the ribbon connection
Why this matters
Geometry establishes the surreal premise
Warning — common failure
Adding glow before placement is correct
Repair it
Hide effects and solve size, overlap and anchor point first
Step 7

Neutralize the old balloon colour

Masked Black & White or Hue/Saturation adjustment

Hotkey: No required shortcut — use the named menu or panel.

Expected result
Pink contamination is removed while useful highlight/shadow information remains
Why this matters
Residual colour reveals the construction
Warning — common failure
Painting gray destructively
Repair it
Use a clipped adjustment and editable mask
Step 8

Isolate the chair

Select Subject/manual tools > Select and Mask > output to mask

Hotkey: W

Expected result
Chair can be adjusted independently with intact thin supports
Why this matters
Foreground separation allows local light and shadow control
Warning — common failure
Accepting a one-click edge
Repair it
Inspect legs, gaps and supports at 100 percent
Step 9

Rebuild the contact shadow

New Multiply layer under chair

Hotkey: B

Expected result
A darker, harder shadow touches each grounded leg
Why this matters
Contact shadow establishes surface contact
Warning — common failure
Using a floating blurred halo
Repair it
Paint at the contact points first
Step 10

Direct the cast shadow

Separate shadow layer > Transform

Hotkey: Cmd/Ctrl T

Expected result
Shadow projects away from the Moon and softens with distance
Why this matters
Direction and falloff reveal the light source
Warning — common failure
Pointing the shadow toward the Moon
Repair it
Draw a light-direction arrow before transforming
Step 11

Create the day-for-night grade

Color Lookup/Curves/Color Balance adjustment layers

Hotkey: No required shortcut — use the named menu or panel.

Expected result
Environment becomes cooler and darker while the Moon retains separation
Why this matters
A global colour world unifies the scene
Warning — common failure
Applying the grade destructively
Repair it
Use adjustments and exclude the Moon with masks
Student check

Answer before moving on

Checkpoint: Midpoint screenshot shows named chair isolation, contact shadow, cast shadow, global grade and at least two local moonlight layers.
Support

Use the provided light-direction overlay and complete contact shadow plus one local-light zone before additional polish.

Transfer

Introduce a weak secondary warm light and reconcile the two-source shadow logic.
