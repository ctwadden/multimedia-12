A Scene is a main-timeline section in Animate. A shot is one uninterrupted camera view. One Scene may contain several shots. This project begins with one shot per Scene so that story and file structure remain manageable.

If you created HTML5 Canvas, create the correct document now and import the artwork again. Do not assume that changing publish settings changes document type.

Diagnosis connects visual choices to audience understanding. Your evidence identifies a visible problem, one repair and a viewer response after the repair.
