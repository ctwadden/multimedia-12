Shot brief

Eight seconds in a canyon.

Deliver a chase shot: a fighter pursues an X-wing through a canyon and is shot down by a second X-wing. Build it in Blender, finish it in After Effects, then make the change your director asks for.

  • Your three pass folders (beauty, mist, glow)
  • A screenshot of your After Effects layer stack
  • The finished MP4 and the director’s version
  • A three-decision explanation and credits

Industry connection

Layout, FX, lighting, comp.

On a film this shot passes through four departments. Layout flies the ships and the camera. FX adds lasers and destruction. Lighting renders the passes. Compositing builds the final picture. You do all four, in that order, and the hand-off between them (the passes) is the part most beginners never see.

Knowledge first

Why this works.

Open the theory deck for the narrated version with checkpoint questions. In short:

One path, many riders

A curve plus Follow Path constraints turns a chase into a few numbers that stay in step.

Speed is things passing close

Low between walls, banking, a camera that aims ahead, motion blur.

Passes

Beauty is the picture. Mist is distance. Lights-off is everything that glows.

Blend modes and mattes

Add and Screen add light. A luma matte lets one picture decide where another shows.

Design principles in the shot

Movement: everything leads the eye down the canyon. Contrast: green and red bolts against orange rock. Emphasis: the flash and fireball on the hit.

02

Draw the flight path

IN SHORTTop view; add Path; Edit Mode; move and extrude points; raise to 17 m and weave; Tilt at bends.
READ ALOUD
DOCUMENT: Your dogfight file
SELECT: A new Path curve
TOOLS / ROUTE: Shift+A › Curve › Path · Tab · G · E · Ctrl+T
  1. Press Numpad 7 (top view). Shift+A › Curve › Path. Rename it Flight path (F2).
  2. Press Tab (Edit Mode). Select all (A), rotate 90° (R Z 90) and scale up (S 60) so it runs along the canyon.
  3. Move points with G so the curve sits in the middle of the canyon floor. Select the end point and press E to extrude new points until the path runs the whole canyon: about 30 points, one every 60 m.
  4. Switch to side view (Numpad 3) and lift all points to about 17 m high (A, then G Z 17). Wobble a few up and down by 5 m, and weave the line about 10 m left and right so it passes close to the rock spires.
  5. For each bend: select the points in it, open the sidebar (N) › Item, and set Tilt to between −45° and 45° so ships lean into the turn. Press Tab to leave Edit Mode.

Action by action: what to click (orange boxes), and what you should see

1Shift+A › Curve › Path.

Wrong vs right

What went wrong when we built the demo: our first glow, made from the whole picture, turned the frame white twice. Our first lights-off pass also made the planet glow, because its material has a little emission: hide it.
STEP CHECKLIST
GLOSSARY
Mist pass: A grey image of distance from the camera
Pass: One layer of information from a render
WHYMist tells the compositor how far away each pixel is. The lights-off pass isolates everything that glows, so glow can be added to those things only.
CHECKdog/mist: grey frames, darker near the camera. dog/glow: black frames with only bolts, engines and flashes.
FIXMist frames all white? Depth is too small: 1400 m. Glow frames show the canyon? A light is still on, or World Strength is not 0.

Wrong vs right

STEP CHECKLIST
GLOSSARY
Solid: A layer of one flat colour
Luma matte: Uses another layer’s brightness to decide where a layer shows
WHYAir scatters light, so distant things look paler. That is how the eye judges scale: without haze, a 1.8 km canyon looks like a model.
