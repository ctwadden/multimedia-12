Success criteria · Sprint 3 campaign

These describe strong work. They are not a mark scheme. Your teacher’s approved rubric will be shared when it is confirmed.

  • My photographs are original, in focus where it matters and exposed so important detail is kept.
  • I changed a capture setting or light on purpose and can explain what it did, using evidence I can see.
  • My edits are reversible, recorded, and do not mislead the viewer about what is true.
  • My campaign’s hierarchy makes the message readable within five seconds.
  • In a new format, on my own, I kept the message and reading order, and I can explain how.
  • My source and consent log is complete.
