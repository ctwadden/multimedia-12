Rescue Run

A 30-second film in six shots, all in one world. Everyone builds the core shot and the edit. Then each group takes one more shot, and the class cuts them into one film.

  1. Core: Canyon Dogfight, then The Cut.
  2. Your group’s shot: pick one of the others.
  3. Every workbook starts with a short theory deck and ends with a director’s change you solve on your own.
