Shot brief

Put the pilot in the fighter.

You are given green-screen footage of a pilot in a studio chair, shot on a moving camera. Deliver a shot where she sits inside a 3D fighter flying above the clouds, then a change of the director’s choosing.

You will hand in

  • Your solve log (error under 1 px)
  • Screenshots of your key tree and your driver
  • The finished shot as an MP4, and the director’s version
  • A three-decision explanation and credits

Industry connection

How the cockpit shots are really made.

Studios film actors in a cockpit set or a chair on a stage, track the camera, and build the rest in 3D. Matchmove artists solve the camera, compositors pull the key, and lighting artists make the CG match. You do all three jobs here.

Knowledge first

Why this works.

Camera solving

Markers that stay still in the real world move across the frame as the camera moves. From that movement Blender works out the camera’s path and lens.

Real scale

Floor, origin and scale turn the solve into a world with real metres, so a 12 m ship is the right size around a real person.

Mattes multiply

A key and a hand-drawn mask multiplied together keep only what both agree on.

A card at the right distance

A picture that fills the frame at 1 m still fills it at 3 m if it is 3 times bigger. A driver keeps the card at her distance, so 3D parts in front of it hide her correctly.

Design principles in the shot

Emphasis: the camera rises to her face. Colour: warm light against a cool sky. Movement: sliding clouds and thrusters.

Place markers and track them

IN SHORTCtrl+click markers; Ctrl+T; refill as the shot changes; delete bad tracks.
READ ALOUD
DOCUMENT: Your clip
SELECT: Markers on the tape triangles on the walls and floor
TOOLS / ROUTE: Ctrl+click to add · Track › Track Markers (Ctrl+T) · backwards Shift+Ctrl+T
  1. Go to frame 1. Ctrl+click the corner of a tape triangle to add a marker. Add 10–12, spread over the walls and the floor.
  2. Do not put markers on the pilot: she moves, so she would ruin the solve. The stands are fine because they stay still.
  3. Select all markers (A) and press Ctrl+T to track forwards. Watch: a marker turns red when it loses its spot.
  4. The camera moves from a wide shot to a close-up, so triangles leave the frame. Every 40 frames or so, add new markers where there are fewer than 8 and track them backwards (Shift+Ctrl+T) and forwards.
  5. Delete any track that jumps to a different triangle (select it, X).

Action by action: what to click (orange boxes), and what you should see

1Frame 150: this is what a tracked shot looks like. Each marker has a short red/blue path. The sidebar Track panel shows the selected marker’s pattern.

Solve the camera

READ ALOUD
DOCUMENT: Your tracks
SELECT: Solve tab in the toolbar
TOOLS / ROUTE: Solve tab · Keyframe A / B · Refine Focal Length · Solve Camera Motion

06

Remove the junk with a garbage mask, and render the key

IN SHORTMask mode; draw; keyframe every 30 frames; Mask × Matte; Set Alpha Apply Mask; render PNG RGBA.
READ ALOUD
DOCUMENT: pilot_key.blend
SELECT: A mask around the pilot and seat
TOOLS / ROUTE: Image Editor › Mask mode · Add › Input › Mask · Math (Multiply) · Set Alpha (Apply Mask) · Render › Render Animation
  1. Change an editor to the Image Editor, show the plate, and switch its mode to Mask. Click New.
  2. Ctrl+click about 12 points around her and the seat, leaving a gap. Press Alt+C to close the shape.
  3. The camera moves, so the mask must move too: with the mouse over the editor press I to key the mask shape, go 30 frames on, move the points, press I again. Repeat to frame 309.
  4. In the compositor add Mask (your mask), a Math node set to Multiply (Keying Matte × Mask), and Set Alpha with Type Apply Mask (Image from Keying, Alpha from Math). Connect Set Alpha to the Group Output.
  5. Output: PNG, RGBA, path //key/ws_. Render › Render Animation. You get 309 transparent PNGs.

Action by action: what to click (orange boxes), and what you should see

1Image Editor in Mask mode: a loose shape around her and the seat.

2Mask node, Math (Multiply), Set Alpha (Apply Mask).

3Output properties: 1920 × 1080, PNG, RGBA, path //key/ws_.

What went wrong when we built the demo: our demo’s garbage matte was computed from the footage instead of hand-drawn, to save time. The drawn, keyframed mask above does the same job; expect it to take most of a lesson.
STEP CHECKLIST
GLOSSARY
Garbage mask: A rough shape that throws away everything outside it
RGBA: Colour plus alpha (transparency)
WHYMattes multiply: she survives only where the key and your mask are both white. Rendering to PNG with alpha gives the 3D scene a clean pilot to place.
CHECK309 PNGs in key/, showing only the pilot and the seat on transparency.
FIXStands flicker back in? A mask keyframe is missing there: add one. Ghost of the stands? You used Replace Alpha: use Apply Mask.
WORKED EXAMPLE · DIFFERENT OBJECTGarbage masks are drawn loose: they only need to keep the junk out, not trace the person.
STRETCH (AFTER THE CHECK PASSES)Feather the mask edge (Mask node › Feather) so it never cuts her hair.
Method basis: B3 · B2. Values and sequence are authored for this project.

TOOLS / ROUTE: Add › Mesh › Circle (Fill N-Gon) · Add › Mesh › Cone · Compositing › Add › Filter › Glare
  1. In each of the four nozzles add a Circle (Fill Type N-Gon) with an Emission material: pink-orange, Strength 40.
  2. Add a Cone behind each nozzle pointing backwards: the plume. Material: Texture Coordinate (Generated) → Separate XYZ (Z) → Math Subtract (1 − Z) → Math Power (2.2) → Factor of a Mix Shader between Transparent and Emission (pink, Strength 14). Render Method Blended.
  3. Parent all eight to the Fighter empty, so the wingmen copies get them too.
  4. Compositing: Render Layers → Glare (Bloom, High, Strength 0.6, Size 0.6) → Group Output.

GLOSSARY
Bloom: Glow that spreads from the brightest parts of an image
Mix Shader: Blends two shaders by a factor
WHYBright emission plus bloom is how glowing light looks on camera. The gradient makes the plume fade instead of ending in a hard edge.
CHECKA rendered frame shows pink thrusters with soft glow, fading plumes, and the hull still crisp.
FIXPlume a solid cone? The Mix factor is the wrong way round: swap the two shader inputs.
