# Decode the Impossible — answer key

## Knowledge check

1. **Black** — Black conceals; white reveals.
2. **PSD** — PSD preserves the editable Photoshop structure.
3. **Touch the nearby surface** — A contact shadow is small and sits where surfaces nearly meet.
4. **To reveal edge contamination** — Different values and colours expose halos or missing detail.
5. **Contradictory depth evidence** — A crossing edge comes forward while a tucked edge goes behind the frame.

## Transfer and defence prompts

1. **Why can a technically clean mask still produce an unbelievable composite?**
   - Listen for: A clean edge cannot repair incompatible viewpoint, scale, perspective, light or resolution.
2. **What should be solved before glow is added?**
   - Listen for: The Moon must first inherit the balloon's size, overlap, distortion and ribbon connection.
3. **How do contact and cast shadows do different jobs?**
   - Listen for: Contact shadow anchors touching surfaces; cast shadow shows light direction and distance.
4. **What proves that the Moon is acting as a light source?**
   - Listen for: Nearby chair surfaces receive directional local light while shadows project away and soften with distance.
5. **What should the final revision target?**
   - Listen for: The weakest diagnosed system, not an unrelated extra effect.
6. **What must remain editable?**
   - Listen for: Masks, Smart Objects, adjustment layers, type and lighting/shadow layers.
7. **Why is a silent five-second test useful?**
   - Listen for: It records received message and hierarchy before the designer explains the intent.
8. **What does the live edit prove?**
   - Listen for: That the student understands and owns the layered construction.

Accept alternate wording when the student uses accurate evidence and can demonstrate the decision.
