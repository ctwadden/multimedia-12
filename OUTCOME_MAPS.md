# Outcome Maps

Official wording is reproduced in part for non-commercial classroom planning with acknowledgement to the Nova Scotia Department of Education and Early Childhood Development.

## Communication Technology 11

Source: `references/NS-Communications-Technology-11-Outcomes-2015.pdf`

| Outcome | Lesson connection | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1.4 determine and create solutions to design problems that can persuade or entertain an audience using a variety of electronic communications tools | Students decode persuasive visual messages and create “Step Outside.” | Gallery discussion; PSD/JPG. |
| 1.5 demonstrate an understanding of the social and environmental impacts of communications technology | Students discuss manipulation, deceptive editing, digital wellbeing, and environmental campaigns. | Ethical prompt; quiz Q6; exit ticket. |
| 4.1 demonstrate an understanding of the principles and elements of design | Students use contrast, emphasis, scale, colour repetition, alignment, and hierarchy. | Exemplar comparison; product. |
| 4.3 demonstrate an understanding of appropriate uses of typography for graphic design | Students create separate headline/subline roles and evaluate legibility/hierarchy. | Type layers; checkpoint 8. |
| 4.4 communicate a message by manipulating images and words using a digital format | Students combine phone, forest, mask, adjustment, type, and shape; Tier 2 adds a refined subject mask and an out-of-bounds visual metaphor. | Layered v01/v02 PSD + exported JPG. |
| 4.5 use appropriate settings for output of images for web use | Students export same-size sRGB JPG at quality 80–90 while preserving the PSD. | Exported JPG + explanation. |

The lesson also begins 1.3 (professional portfolio) through process evidence and 1.7 (safe practices) through responsible file handling and ethical image use.

## Multimedia 12

Source: `references/NS-Multimedia-12-Outcomes-2015.pdf`

| Outcome | Lesson connection | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 apply techniques and procedures needed to manipulate images (including text) in a range of media, including digital and electronic media | Students place, transform, select, refine edges, paint on masks, manage layer order, adjust, typeset, save, and export. | PSD layer/mask inspection; performance checkpoints. |
| 1.2 demonstrate an understanding of the cultural, historical, and emotional impact of other people’s images by examining their form and content | Students analyze advertising and social-issue visual metaphors. | Gallery responses; discussion. |
| 1.3 demonstrate an awareness of the procedures involved in the production of images in a range of digital and electronic media | Two simulators and tiered builds expose the layer-by-layer workflow from simple portal to out-of-bounds composite. | Technique explanation; v01/v02 process screenshots. |
| 1.4 apply principles of art and design to create digital and electronic images | Students apply hierarchy, emphasis, alignment, contrast, colour, and scale. | Final product + explanation. |
| 1.5 construct digital and electronic images which communicate ideas and concepts | “Step Outside” communicates a digital-wellbeing concept. | JPG plus editable PSD. |
| 4.2 demonstrate an understanding of the cultural, historical, and emotional impact of other people’s multimedia products by examining their form and content, audience and purpose | Students use message/audience/evidence/ethics prompts with real campaigns. | Conversation and exit ticket. |

## Day 1 assessment boundary

This is a foundations sequence and should be assessed formatively. Tier 1 supplies the common baseline; Tier 2 deepens masking and layer order; Tier 3 transfers the method. Strong evidence is successful workflow and explanation, not professional edge quality on a first attempt.
